## Key Takeaways
- **The industry is diverse:** You don't just have to inspect abandoned houses; there are luxury commercial gigs and simple delivery gigs.
- **Pay scales widely:** Entry-level drive-bys pay $15, while commercial loss control can pay $400.
- **Stacking is key:** The best inspectors belong to 15+ different vendor panels simultaneously to keep their routes dense.

<h2 id="mortgage">1-3: Mortgage Default & Foreclosure</h2>

When people hear "field inspector," this is usually what they picture.

1. **The Drive-By Inspector (Occupancy Check):** You drive by a house, take 4 photos from your car, and answer whether someone lives there. *Pay: $15 - $25.*
2. **The Property Preservation Contractor:** You actually physically secure the home. You change locks, board windows, and pump out flooded basements. *Pay: $50 - $1,500+ depending on the work order.*
3. **The Letter Deliverer (Contact Inspector):** The bank legally has to try and contact a borrower. You tape an envelope to the door and take a photo. *Pay: $20 - $40.*

<h2 id="commercial">4-6: Commercial & Loss Control</h2>

If you want to step away from residential foreclosures, the commercial side is highly lucrative.

4. **Insurance Loss Control Inspector:** Insurance companies hire you to evaluate a business's risk before issuing a policy. You look for fire hazards, proper exits, and liability risks. *Pay: $75 - $250.*
5. **Draw Inspector (Construction):** When a builder is building a new subdivision, the bank won't release the next chunk of money until you go out and verify the drywall is actually installed. *Pay: $50 - $150.*
6. **Commercial Site Verifier:** Checking on leased equipment, verifying franchise standards, or conducting leased-vehicle condition reports. *Pay: $60 - $120.*

<h2 id="niche">7-10: Niche & Specialty</h2>

7. **Disaster Relief Inspector (FEMA):** Deployed after hurricanes or floods to assess structural damage rapidly. *Pay: Extremely high, but erratic.*
8. **High-Value Asset Inspector:** Verifying the existence and condition of luxury assets (yachts, fleet trucks) before they are used as collateral. *Pay: $100 - $300.*
9. **Mystery Shopper (Audit):** Operating under the guise of a standard customer, you evaluate brand compliance at retail locations on behalf of corporate. *Pay: $30 - $100 + reimbursements.*
10. **Trashout Specialist:** Specifically contracted just to haul junk and debris from evicted properties. Heavy labor, but highly profitable per hour. *Pay: $500 - $2,000+ per job.*
