<SectionHeading id="the-math">Why the Math Demands Routing Software</SectionHeading>

As a mortgage field inspector or property preservation contractor, you are not paid by the hour. You are paid by the completed work order. The moment you leave your driveway, your primary enemy is "windshield time." 

If you are completing 30 occupancy drive-by orders a day at $12 an order, you are making $360 roughly on the day. But if you spend 5 hours driving to complete them because your path criss-crossed the same highway three times, you are effectively bleeding margins to fuel and fatigue. 

By forcing those exact 30 addresses into a mathematical optimization sequence, an algorithm can map them strictly in a radial circle that slashes your driving time by up to 35%. 

<Callout type="warning" title="Google Maps is Dead for Volume">
Google Maps is fundamentally built to take a user from Point A to Point B. It restricts multiple stops strictly to 10 nodes, and heavily resists algorithmic re-ordering. Stop trying to chunk 60 orders manually into Google Maps links.
</Callout>

<SectionHeading id="the-leaders">The Industry Leaders</SectionHeading>

While there are dozens of generic delivery apps built specifically for Amazon Flex or FedEx drivers, field inspectors have unique requirements.

<InfoStack>
  <InfoBlock title="Circuit Route Planner">
    **The Current Favorite.** Circuit allows you to snap a photo of a printed manifest or upload a CSV provided by your field service company, and it instantly blasts out the ultimate route sequence. Features voice capabilities to mark "delivered/completed" hands-free.
  </InfoBlock>
  <InfoBlock title="RoadWarrior">
    **The Budget King.** An incredibly reliable fallback that has deeply integrated support for FedEx routing but translates flawlessly to mortgage inspections. Highly customizable time-windows for when you must hit specific properties.
  </InfoBlock>
  <InfoBlock title="Route4Me">
    **The Enterprise Option.** Slightly overkill for the solo contractor, but if you run an LLC structure and manage two or three junior sub-inspectors beneath you, Route4Me allows you to deploy and distribute master routes straight to their phones automatically.
  </InfoBlock>
</InfoStack>

<SectionHeading id="execution">Workflow Automation Strategy</SectionHeading>

Your morning workflow needs to be algorithmic. Attempting to manually type 40 unique addresses into a phone at 6:00 AM while drinking coffee leads to manual entry errors that send you 12 miles in the wrong direction.

<StepCard number={1} title="Export">
Export your daily manifest directly from your national preservation portal (like InspectorADE or EZInspections) directly as an Excel or CSV file.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Inject">
Upload the raw file into Circuit on your desktop or via the mobile app. Let the app sequence the route in under roughly 15 seconds.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Execute">
Drive. You should only have to click "next stop" inside the ecosystem. The engine will bridge natively into Google Maps or Apple Maps, guide you natively to the property, allow you to shoot your photos, and repeat the hand-off.
</StepCard>
