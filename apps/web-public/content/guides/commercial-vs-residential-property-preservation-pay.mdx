<SectionHeading id="the-pay-gap">The Pay Gap: Real Numbers</SectionHeading>

If you're asking "Which pays more?", the answer depends on whether you look at the **Check** or the **Day**.

Commercial preservation represents the "big ticket" side of the industry. While a standard residential winterization might pay $100, a commercial rooftop HVAC winterization on a 50,000 sq ft warehouse can pay $1,500+. 

Here is the average per-order breakdown from our 2026 vendor data:

| Work Order Type | Residential Average | Commercial Average |
|---|---|---|
| **Initial Securing** | $50 - $150 | $300 - $800 |
| **Winterization** | $75 - $125 | $400 - $1,500 |
| **Condition Report** | $25 - $75 | $150 - $400 |
| **Lawn Maintenance** | $35 - $80 | $200 - $1,000+ |
| **Debris Removal** | $35/yd | $50/yd + equipment fees |

<SectionHeading id="volume-vs-value">Volume vs Value</SectionHeading>

The catch is **Density**. 

In a high-volume residential territory (like suburban Atlanta or Houston), you might have 15 work orders within a 5-mile radius. You can finish your day by 2:00 PM and gross $600.

In commercial work, you may have to drive 60 miles for a single $500 order. You will spend 3 hours on-site, deal with complex security gates, and navigate an 80-page reporting requirement. 

<Callout type="tip" title="The Hybrid Strategy">
The most profitable firms in 2026 use a hybrid strategy: they maintain a 'dense' residential crew that handles the neighborhood volume, while the owner or a senior lead handles the 'high-value' commercial site visits 2 to 3 days per week.
</Callout>

<SectionHeading id="transition">How to Transition to Commercial</SectionHeading>

Don't jump cold. Use your residential success to build a bridge.

<StepCard number={1} title="Audit Your Insurance">
Standard residential liability ($500k) won't cut it. Most commercial banks require a minimum of $1,000,000 or $2,000,000 in general liability plus specialized E&O.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Target Small 'Strip Center' Portfolios">
Start with small commercial—local strip malls, small office suites. The systems are similar to residential but the pay is weighted toward commercial rates.
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Update Your Profile in the Directory">
Ensure your hiring portal profiles explicitly check 'Commercial Capable'. Many firms filter by this checkbox first when high-value orders land.
</StepCard>
