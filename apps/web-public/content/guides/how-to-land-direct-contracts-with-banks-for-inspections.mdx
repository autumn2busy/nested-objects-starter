<SectionHeading id="the-truth">The Truth About Direct Bank Contracts</SectionHeading>

If you are a vendor in the property preservation and field inspection space, hitting the ceiling on national order payouts gets old fast. Completing an interior occupancy check for a national firm for $15 is reasonable for volume padding, but knowing that the national firm billed the bank $45 for your exact photos starts a burning question in every inspector's mind:

"How do I just cut them out and work direct?"

Landing direct inspection contracts specifically points to establishing yourself as the "prime vendor" with a lending institution. It represents a dramatic jump in per-order revenue—but fundamentally changes your business structure from an independent contractor to an enterprise vendor firm.

<Callout type="info" title="The Hierarchy of Mortage Field Services">
The banking supply chain requires someone to accept systemic liability. Lenders contract out massive volumes to National Firms specifically so they don't have to deal with thousands of 1099 contractors. The National Firm's entire job is to hold liability and distribute orders. 
</Callout>

<SectionHeading id="nationals-vs-direct">National Firms vs Direct Bank Contracts</SectionHeading>

When you sign up directly through a lender, you absorb the risk, you dictate the SLA (Service Level Agreement), and you retain 100% of the margins.

| Feature | National Firms (Subcontracting) | Direct Bank Contracts (Prime) |
|---|---|---|
| **Per-Order Payout** | Low to Medium | Very High (100% margin) |
| **Volume Reliability** | Extremely High | Varies by default rates |
| **Payment terms** | 15 to 30 Days (Net-30) | Up to 60 or 90 Days (Net-60) |
| **Liability required** | Basic Liability | Severe (E&O, Workers Comp, General) |
| **Software fees** | Free / Provided by them | You purchase the VMS integration |

<SectionHeading id="requirements">Non-Negotiable Direct Requirements</SectionHeading>

You cannot approach a localized credit union or private capital firm with a Gmail address and expect vendor approval. To become a Prime, you have to look, act, and secure like a prime.

<RequirementsGrid>
  <RequirementCard>LLC or S-Corp Registration in good standing.</RequirementCard>
  <RequirementCard>General Liability Insurance ($1,000,000+ minimum).</RequirementCard>
  <RequirementCard>Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurance.</RequirementCard>
  <RequirementCard>Aspen iRecord (Aspen Grove) background check compliance.</RequirementCard>
  <RequirementCard>Capital reserves to front field-preservation material costs.</RequirementCard>
  <RequirementCard>A Professional domain and Vendor packet.</RequirementCard>
</RequirementsGrid>

<SectionHeading id="how-to-find">How to Find Banks that Hire Directly</SectionHeading>

Stop trying to contact JPMorgan Chase. Massive banks do not want individual contractors; they want national aggregates who can absorb volume across all 50 states.

<StepCard number={1} title="Target Regional and Community Banks">
Regional banks and localized credit unions maintain smaller, highly localized mortgage portfolios. Because they operate at smaller scales, maintaining an ultra-expensive contract with a National Field Services giant eats into their profit margins. They **want** local contractors. Search "Credit unions in [My County]".
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Target Hard Money Lenders">
Private equity groups and Hard Money lenders finance fix-and-flips and commercial acquisitions. Because these loans are incredibly high risk, these lenders need rapid, direct oversight on their collateral. You can contact them directly to offer "Collateral Draw Inspections".
</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Target Property Management Firms for REO">
When local banks successfully foreclose, the property enters an REO (Real Estate Owned) status. Many banks utilize independent local Property Management brokerages to manage these assets locally until they are liquidated.
</StepCard>

<SectionHeading id="execution">Pitching and Execution</SectionHeading>

You will need a **Vendor Packet**. This is a PDF document that outlines your coverage area by Zip Code, details your SLA (for example, "All occupancy checks submitted within 24 hours of dispatch"), provides a strict, locked pricing matrix, and attaches your certificates of Insurance (COI) and W-9.

Do not email standard real estate agents. Determine the bank or credit union's "Asset Manager" or "Loss Mitigation Director" via LinkedIn. 

<TipStack>
  <TipCard title="Pricing Strategy">
    Do not underbid yourself into a corner. Direct contracts expect premium speed and white-glove accuracy, and they are willing to pay corresponding pricing.
  </TipCard>
  <TipCard title="Follow up consistently">
    Lenders might not need a new vendor the week you cold call them, but if their current prime vendor misses a critical securing SLA and the property is legally compromised, your number needs to be in their rolodex. 
  </TipCard>
</TipStack>
