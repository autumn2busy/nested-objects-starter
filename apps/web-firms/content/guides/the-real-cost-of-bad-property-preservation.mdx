## Key Takeaways
- **Fines Compound:** A single overgrown lawn can lead to municipal fines exceeding the property's tax value if ignored.
- **Water Damage is Fatal:** Failing to properly drain pipes (winterization) causes hundreds of thousands in mold and structural damage.
- **Chargebacks Hurt You:** If you are the inspector who missed the damage, the hiring firm will likely charge you back for the violation.

<h2 id="code-violations">Municipal Code Violations</h2>

When a property goes into default and the borrower vacates, it instantly becomes a liability for the neighborhood. Cities and HOAs do not care if a property is caught in a legal foreclosure battle; they care if the grass is two feet high or if the pool is breeding mosquitoes.

If a field inspector marks a property as "maintained" when it's not, the city will issue fines. Some municipalities issue daily recurring fines of $500 until the violation is cured. By the time the bank realizes what happened, they could owe $15,000 for tall grass. 

<h2 id="weather-damage">Failure to Winterize</h2>

The number one costliest mistake in property preservation is a botched winterization. 

When a property is vacant in a cold climate, the plumbing system must be completely drained, blown out with an air compressor, and filled with non-toxic antifreeze. If an inspector fakes the report (or does it incorrectly), the pipes will freeze and burst. Once spring arrives and the ice thaws, the entire home will flood, leading to catastrophic mold and foundation issues.
