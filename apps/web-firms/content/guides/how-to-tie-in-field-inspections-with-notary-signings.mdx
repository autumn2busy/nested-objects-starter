## Key Takeaways
- **Efficiency:** You are already driving to specific neighborhoods; stacking an exterior inspection with a notary signing maximizes your dollar-per-mile.
- **Trust factor:** Firms highly value commissioned notaries for field services because of their proven background checks and professionalism.
- **Double Billing:** Some mortgage lenders require a drive-by inspection *and* a loan signing for a single property, allowing you to capture both fees.

<h2 id="the-synergy">The Synergy: Why Notaries Make Great Inspectors</h2>

For years, the mobile notary and field inspection industries operated in silos. However, as the gig economy matures into 2026, **the most successful independent contractors are merging the two.**

If you are already a commissioned notary public, you have the hardest part of vendor contracting out of the way: you've passed a rigorous state background check, you understand confidentiality (GLBA compliance), and you know how to operate on the road.

Field service firms immediately prioritize vendor applications from active notaries because it reduces their onboarding risk. 

<h2 id="how-to-stack">How to Stack Your Services</h2>

Rather than choosing one lane, here is how you can operate as a hybrid vendor:

1. **The Route Optimization Play:** When accepting a loan signing appointment at 2:00 PM, log into your field inspection portal and accept 2-3 exterior inspections (drive-bys) in the same zip code.
2. **The "All-In-One" Mortgage Package:** Reach out to mortgage lenders on the Nested Objects directory and offer a bundled rate. When a borrower needs closing docs signed, you can also perform the mandatory exterior property condition report.
3. **Insurance Draw Inspections:** Construction loans require a notary for the lien release out front, and an inspector to verify construction progress in the back. You can do both.

<h2 id="pricing">Pricing Your Hybrid Services</h2>

While a standard drive-by inspection pays $15 to $30, a loan signing pays $75 to $150. By combining them:

- You eliminate dead drive time between notary gigs.
- You can offer a "Premium Rush Service" offering to lenders, charging a base fee of $200 for a dual-service dispatch.
