# Market Analysis & Revenue Projection: Nested Objects

**Date:** December 15, 2025
**Subject:** Launch Strategy & Financial Outlook
**Target Market:** Field Inspection, Mobile Notary, and Gig Economy Services

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Nested Objects is positioned to capitalize on a critical gap in the **$12B+ Field Inspection** and **$2B+ Mobile Notary** markets: the fragmentation of the independent workforce. While demand for on-demand field data (inspections, notarizations, verifications) is growing at **6-7% CAGR**, the workforce remains disorganized, relying on disparate job boards and manual tracking.

By providing a centralized "Business-in-a-Box" platform that offers intelligence, routing, and directory access, Nested Objects addresses the two biggest pain points for this workforce: **finding high-quality work** and **maximizing route efficiency**.

## 2. Market Sizing (USA)

The Total Addressable Market (TAM) represents the intersection of the massive Gig Economy and the specialized Test, Inspection, and Certification (TIC) sector.

*   **TAM (Total Addressable Market): $14 Billion+**
    *   Combined US Field Inspection ($12B) and Mobile Notary ($2B) markets.
    *   Driven by regulatory compliance, real estate stability, and insurance mandates.
*   **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market): $2.5 Billion**
    *   Focus on the **Independent Contractor (1099)** segment of the market.
    *   Estimating ~200,000 full-time equivalent independent inspectors and notaries in the US.
*   **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): $25 Million (Year 1-3)**
    *   Targeting **5% market capture** of active independent pros.
    *   ~10,000 active subscribers.

### Key Trends 2025
*   **Tech-Enabled Gig Work**: The "Uber-fication" of skilled trades. Workers demand apps that aggregate work rather than managed employee relationships.
*   **Remote Online Notarization (RON)**: 18% CAGR in digital notary services drives demand for tech-savvy notaries who need better software tools.
*   **Consolidation of "Side Hustles"**: Gig workers are moving from low-skill delivery (DoorDash) to high-skill field services (Inspections) for better margins ($30-$60/hr vs $15/hr).

## 3. Revenue Model & Pricing Strategy

Nested Objects utilizes a tiered subscription model designed to capture value at every stage of the user's career lifecycle.

| Plan | Price | Annual Value (ARPU) | Target Audience | Strategic Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starter** | **$0** | $0 | New entrants, Browsers | **Lead Magnet**: Low friction acquisition. |
| **Directory** | **$99** / 3mo | $396 | One-off users | **Monetized Churn**: Captures value from short-term users. |
| **Pro** | **$37** / mo | $444 | Working Inspectors | **Core Engine**: High retention, main recurring revenue. |

## 5. Strategic Recommendations

1.  **Kill the "Directory" Plan Churn**: The $99/3mo plan is unique but risky. **Recommendation**: Aggressively upsell Directory users to "Pro" ($37/mo) in Month 2. It offers them more value (AI tools) for a lower perceived monthly cost, increasing LTV.
2.  **Focus on "Pro" as the Hero SKU**: At $37/mo, it is an impulse buy for anyone completing just **one** inspection ($30-$60) per month. Marketing should frame it as "Pays for itself with your first job."
3.  **Agency-Led Acquisition**: Use the $297 Agency plan to acquire users for free. If an Agency brings 10 inspectors, those 10 inspectors eventually become standalone "Pro" users if they leave the agency.
4.  **Mobile-First is Critical**: Field workers are mobile-only. The "Profile Widget" and "Dashboard" refinements we just deployed are critical for conversion.
