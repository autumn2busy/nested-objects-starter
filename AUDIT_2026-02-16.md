### /jobs Page

The `/jobs` page is a **Job Command Center** with two tabs:
1. **Find Jobs** — fetches from `/api/jobs`, displays title/company/location/pay/description/roles, with "Save to tracker" button
2. **My Pipeline** — Kanban-style tracker (Interested -> Applied -> Interview -> Offer) fetching from `/api/member-jobs`

Gated behind `<Gate feature="job_board">` with login/upgrade fallback. Currently has **11 rows** in the `jobs` table.
