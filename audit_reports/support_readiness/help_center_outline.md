# Help Center Outline

## Section 1: Getting Started
1. **Welcome to Nested Objects**: An overview of the hub, its mission, and how to navigate the dashboard.
2. **Setting Up Your Profile**: innovative guide to completing your profile for maximum visibility and networking.
3. **Your First 7 Days**: A step-by-step roadmap for new members to get the most value immediately (based on the "Your first 7 days" section).

## Section 2: Membership & Billing
4. **Understanding Membership Plans**: Detailed comparison of Free, Starter, Pro, Elite, and Agency tiers, highlighting feature differences.
5. **Managing Your Subscription**: How to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan via the billing portal.
6. **Updating Payment Methods**: Instructions for securely updating credit card information through Outseta.
7. **Accessing Invoices**: How to find and download past invoices for tax and expense purposes.

## Section 3: Using the Directory
8. **Searching for Firms**: Tips for using filters (region, pay, tools) to find the right hiring firms.
9. **Saving & Tracking Listings**: How to use the "shortlist" or tracking features to organize your applications.
10. **Understanding Firm Data**: Explanation of the data points provided for each firm (pay rates, requirements) and how they are verified.

## Section 4: AI & Tools
11. **Using the AI Concierge**: Best practices for asking questions and getting instant answers about field inspections.
12. **Building Your Resume**: How to use the AI Resume Builder to create a professional, industry-specific resume.
13. **Understanding AI Limits**: Explanation of monthly credit limits for Starter vs. Pro/Elite plans and how they reset.

## Section 5: Troubleshooting & Support
14. **Login Issues**: Common solutions for password resets, email verification, and browser caching issues.
15. **Reporting Data Errors**: How to flag incorrect firm information or technical bugs to the support team.
