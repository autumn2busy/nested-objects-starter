# FAQ Updates

## Pricing

**Q: What is the difference between the Starter and Pro plans?**
A: The **Starter** plan is designed for those who need access to the directory and training materials but only occasional AI support. It includes limited monthly credits for the AI Concierge and Resume Builder. The **Pro** plan is our "working pro" toolkit (most popular), offering full access to all AI tools, job tracking, routing features, and increased usage limits to support your daily workflow.

**Q: Do I need to pay extra for the AI tools?**
A: No, AI access is included in your membership. Starter plans include a base level of credits suitable for occasional use, while Pro and Elite plans provide significantly higher limits designed for frequent usage.

## Trials

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes! We offer a **7-day risk-free trial** for our Pro plan. You can explore the full directory, test the AI tools, and access training materials completely free for one week. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel at any time during the trial period to avoid being billed.

**Q: What happens after my free trial ends?**
A: If you choose to continue, your payment method will be charged for the Pro plan subscription. If you decide it's not for you, simply cancel before the 7 days are up, and you won't be charged a dime. You can also downgrade to the Free plan to keep preview access.

## Refunds

**Q: What is your refund policy?**
A: We offer a "cancel anytime" policy. If you cancel your subscription, you will retain access to your plan features until the end of your current billing cycle. We do not typically offer refunds for partial months or past usage, but if you believe there has been a billing error, please reach out to our support team immediately.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I forget to cancel my trial?**
A: We send a reminder before your trial ends. generally, we do not refund trial conversions if the service was available to use, but we handle exceptions on a case-by-case basis within 48 hours of the charge. Please contact support if you have a specific issue.
