# Prioritized Content Plan & Gap Analysis
**Focus**: Ranking for "Field Inspector Directory", "Independent Field Inspector", and "Gig Work"
**Strategy**: Capture high-intent traffic from inspectors looking for work and firms looking for inspectors.

## 🔴 Content Gaps Identified
1.  **"Field Inspector Directory"**: The exact phrase is missing from the H1 and Meta Title of the main directory page.
2.  **Local Landing Pages**: No pages targeting "Inspector in [State]" or "Inspector in [City]".
3.  **Role Definitions**: "Mortgage Field Inspector" page exists, but "Insurance Loss Control" is thin.
4.  **Entry-Level Guides**: Missing "No Experience" content which is high volume.
