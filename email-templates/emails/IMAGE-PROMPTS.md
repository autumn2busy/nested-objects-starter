## PRODUCTION NOTES

1. **Aspect Ratio:** All images should be rendered at 600×280px (roughly 2.14:1) for email hero use. Generate at 2x resolution (1200×560) for retina displays.
2. **File Format:** Export as .jpg at 80% quality for email file size optimization. Target under 150KB per image.
3. **Alt Text:** Each email template includes descriptive alt text. Ensure images don't carry critical information that isn't also in the email body.
4. **Color Consistency:** Post-process all images to match the brand palette — teal (#2A7166) and charcoal (#343A40) should be present in every image.
5. **Midjourney Params:** Append `--ar 15:7 --v 6 --style raw` to each prompt for best results.
6. **DALL-E 3:** Use "photographic" style, specify 1792×1024 (closest to 2:1), and post-crop to 600×280.
